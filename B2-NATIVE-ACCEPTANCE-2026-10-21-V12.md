## What V12 adds to the nineteen-case V11b proof

The original nineteen method bodies, strict constructor comparisons, sixteen
native function seals plus the separate anchor broker verification, least-rights
checks, refusal catches, policy and execution limits remain. The SQL accounting
component envelope changes its maximum Boolean field to `false`, which encodes
one byte wider than `true`. Fixture pins and provenance follow that correction.
Five added methods test independent accounting/encoding and controlled races:

- The accounting-component envelope covers both Boolean widths. This is a
  component proof, not a fabricated maximum native business state.
- Independently observed selected row wires/metadata and declared logical keys
  reconcile the installation-plus-Stop vector: **35 retained rows including six
  bootstrap rows; 31 logical writes; 62 declared keys; 33 row touches and 68 key
  touches including staging/deferred updates**. Retained rows, logical credits
  and provisional DML are distinct. These are not physical tuple/index/TOAST/WAL
  measurements, and the expected values do not call `_cost` as their oracle.
- Installation getter closure has three pins; the tested four-direct-fact-pin
  Stop closure has six, including ancestor dependencies. The complete compact
  UTF-8 scalar plus one LF is verified with nested `original_wire` escaping and
  canonical serializer vectors. Summing raw string lengths is not accepted as
  the encoded reply size.
- A concurrent same-original waiter blocks on the scope and later reconciles
  the winner's committed original; it performs no new action under a new ID.
- A parent-SELECT snapshot held across another writer's COMMIT remains a
  coherent earlier absence/head-0 observation; a fresh statement observes the
  committed original/head-1. It cannot mix the two snapshots.

The races use owned transaction/lock barriers, preserving the existing
timeouts. No arbitrary sleeps, source hooks, catalog mutation to fake a race,
anchor UPDATE grant or expanded native authority is introduced.

   demonstrate an admitted maximum.
2. Re-derive full distinct-seven-pin Stop/terminal reserve after the Boolean
   envelope correction, then prove admitted-state capacity and Stop liveness.
   The four-direct-pin path does not accept the seven-pin state, all sixteen
   positions or seventy-pin maximum. The other nine transition kinds still
   refuse until separately implemented and tested.
3. Review a dedicated NOLOGIN production anchor owner and actual managed
   owner/ACL/revocation capability. The laboratory's existing trusted `postgres`
   broker does not prove a deployment-ready owner boundary.
4. Compose the exact candidate against the current accepted source, complete
   applicable ordinary and actual CI checks, and review a separate source

5. Complete capture retention, raw/current-clock admission, commit/replay,
   closure, trusted reader and minute/seal completeness, then restoration,
   retention and UI joins. A per-seal bound or process cache does not bound

   unknown under its original identity.
