## Prepare, record and read back

1. **Prepare once and verify the committed reservation before any provider I/O.** Bind the exact canonical reservation request and digest, the original `PreparedPacket` wire, observation/record IDs, RPC correlation IDs, and the separately preallocated capture, admission, availability and terminal IDs under the reviewed identity registry. Keep the durable scope's one-unresolved-packet guard. Lost reservation acknowledgment requires scoped same-ID readback. If it is absent or unavailable, keep the original unresolved.
2. **Collect once, then retain safe originals in a separate transaction.** The recorder captures Jupiter Price, Solana genesis and exact-block-time responses under the reserved IDs. Preserve the exact safe original capsule and raw bytes, canonical public request, approved response metadata and receipt wall/monotonic clocks. Use `validate_capture` to bind them to the reservation. Persist the canonical Capture command under the preallocated ID with the safe complete or incomplete outcome, without economic admission. The parser's existing `withheld`, `partial_discarded`, and `not_received` dispositions remain evidence-based. A missing body after restart is not evidence that no response arrived. Any response body or even a complete safe capsule that existed only in RAM before the Capture transaction durably committed can be lost. Reservation and hashes cannot reconstruct those bytes or clocks.
3. **Read through the independent scoped getter before release.** Resolve the exact original command, result, receipt and all raw/capsule/dependency pins. Require matching scope, operation kind, original ID and command bytes/hash. A provisional writer reply, cached copy, or partial dependency page is insufficient. If eligible, a separate original admission uses retained evidence and genuine current clock/head/fence checks. Its actual committed visibility must be read independently and retained as the original Availability with its real UTC upper bound. If non-admitted, only the reviewed separately versioned scope-locked terminal action establishes terminal absence. Reconcile that action by its exact original ID. A capture-only result and an absent getter remain distinct from terminal closure. Cache release needs a durable reconciled terminal packet and the exact acknowledged hashes. Keep identities, raw originals and required history/seal pins after releasing the one matching in-memory entry.

## Three crash tests for the same original

| Fault position | Required recovery result |
| --- | --- |
| Reservation committed, then interruption before durable Capture, including after HTTP completes in memory | Fresh reader resolves the reservation. The capture getter may return `observed_absent_unknown`. Preserve uncertainty and block another packet. When exact bodies were lost, retain no fabricated capsules or transport outcome. If authority permits closure, commit only the preallocated terminal tombstone after locked reconciliation with any delayed writer. |
| Capture COMMIT sent and writer response lost before admission | An independent fresh reader resolves the exact capture original and all original bytes. No provider reacquisition. A capture with unresolved COMMIT stays pending. Only a verified committed capture can feed a separately authorized original admission, subject to existing age and head guards. Expired retained evidence is still historical, with no refreshed receipts. |
| Admission COMMIT or original Availability acknowledgment lost | Resolve the original admission and prepared Availability by their original IDs. Reuse the exact retained reader observation if present. If no original observation has been prepared or recorded and the state is independently resolved, an authorized first observation uses its actual later bound. Never replace an uncertain observation or backdate minute eligibility. Refuse cache release until the exact outcome and dependencies reconcile. |

Assert no extra HTTP call or allocated original, no duplicate journal action, no synthesized body or receipt and no ordinary admission while the original is unknown. Getters and exact replay must preserve committed rows. Run the delayed-writer versus terminal closure race under the same lock and prove either one original commit or one permanent closure. A negative getter result or timeout alone permits neither guessed same-byte resubmission nor replacement identities.
