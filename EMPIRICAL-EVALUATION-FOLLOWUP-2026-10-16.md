## 1. Freeze an experiment family, not just each successful run

**Existing:** the twenty-point reference GA/random comparison already shares initialization, charges duplicate logical requests, separates exhaustive coverage cost, preserves complete artifacts and selects only on training. The paired wrapper freezes seeds/budgets and explicitly treats seeds on one path as algorithm variability. Reimplementing those controls would add little.

**New deliverable:** an append-only experiment-family registry linking every prepared/failed/completed run, searched domain, objective/risk/cost variant, initialization rule, source/parser version, data window, adaptive choice and holdout access. Predeclare the primary comparison and stopping rule; record why runs are excluded without deleting their denominator. Once inspected, a holdout becomes research data for subsequent choices. Separate market windows from seed repetitions when reporting uncertainty; neither eight candidates nor thirty seeds creates thirty independent markets.

Make the optimizer interface/domain explicit before claiming the operational solver beats random search. The reference grid has twenty pairs. The selected integer ranges fast 3–12 and slow 15–60 contain 460 legal pairs. Compare the actual solver and random over that same declared domain, capital/risk lifecycle, initialization and request budget, with the grid separately charged; alternatively retain the twenty-point result's restricted-reference label. Keep logical requests, unique evaluations, cache reuse and measured runtime separate. [Bergstra and Bengio](https://jmlr.org/papers/v13/bergstra12a.html) motivate random search as a baseline; their neural-network results do not establish trading performance.

Keep DSR/PBO unavailable until their inputs and assumptions are reviewed. [Deflated Sharpe Ratio](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf) requires return-series/sample-moment and trial-selection information; its independent-trial count is not simply the twenty candidates or seed count. A correlation-based effective count is an estimate, particularly fragile with short samples. Do not apply DSR to the current ROI-minus-drawdown fitness. [PBO/CSCV](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf) needs synchronous performance series across alternatives and a metric estimable on subsamples. Its combinatorial partitions are a retrospective selection diagnostic, not automatically a chronological deployment simulation. Drawdown objectives and stateful trading need explicit seam/reset treatment before a CSCV result is meaningful.

**Acceptance:** a reviewer can enumerate all choices/exclusions, reproduce every selected result and its control, identify holdout contamination, and see either justified statistical-method eligibility or an explicit insufficient-evidence result. Preserve current fitness and cash abstention; a new objective needs a fresh protocol.

## 2. Audit time intervals and quantify initialization sensitivity

**Existing:** fresh scoring accounts, boundary liquidation, causal indicator prefixes, completed bars, later eligible execution quotes and prefix-invariance tests already address important leakage paths. The 915-minute/61-bar readiness convention is explicit; it is not an empirical convergence guarantee.

**New deliverable:** a split manifest recording, for each scored interval, the latest information availability used for selection, indicator history, signal availability, action/fill interval and terminal valuation. Use prespecified forward train/validation windows and a final untouched interval. [Hyndman and Athanasopoulos](https://otexts.com/fpp3/toolbox.html#time-series-cross-validation) describe rolling-origin evaluation using past observations only. [TimeSeriesSplit](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.model_selection.TimeSeriesSplit.html) exposes a sample gap; it does not determine this strategy's economically justified gap.

Do not automatically purge the 61 warmup bars. Past observations legitimately available to a later EMA are different from outcomes spanning a test interval. If future labels, overlapping outcome horizons or nonchronological folds are introduced, remove training observations whose outcome-information intervals overlap testing and justify any additional embargo from those dependencies. The [originator's CPCV description](https://www.quantresearch.org/Innovations.htm) targets leaked training information; no fixed embargo percentage is established for this bot. Account resets must discard positions/intents while preserving only the declared causal indicator state. Shared calendar windows also remain correlated evidence.

There is a useful hand-worked initialization check. Existing `market/candles.py` seeds period-p EMA with the first p-bar SMA, then applies alpha=2/(p+1). The provider path's `db/provider_protocol.py::_seed_ema` likewise sends 61 bars through `core/exact_rehearsal.py::_append_candle`, whose indicator recurrence uses binary64 values serialized as hexadecimal; its exact integer economic ledger is separate. For two initial states followed by identical closes, the difference after k recursive updates is `delta_k=(1-alpha)^k*delta_0` for the ideal unrounded fixed-period recurrence. For slow 60, alpha=2/61, so the residual multiplier is `(59/61)^k`. The seed is created at bar 60; at common bar 61 only k=1 update has occurred, retaining 59/61, approximately 96.7%, of the initial-state difference. Shorter genes have received more recursive updates by that same common boundary.

This does not quantify unknown actual price error, forecast bias or loss: delta_0 is unknown, this is not a bit-exact error bound for the rounded implementation, and a small EMA perturbation can still change a near-equality crossing. As an offline sensitivity experiment, compare the unchanged 61-bar convention with predeclared longer common prefixes ending at identical decision boundaries, for every gene/control. Report EMA differences, crossing/intent/fill changes, rankings and net economics, not just last-indicator error. [Freqtrade's own recursive-analysis documentation](https://www.freqtrade.io/en/stable/recursive-analysis/) provides a useful varying-history check while explicitly distinguishing indicator analysis from strategy backtesting. No installation is needed to borrow that validation idea.

**Acceptance:** temporal-overlap diagnostics and independent long-prefix comparisons are reviewable, including any near-crossing changes. Do not silently replace 915-minute readiness, rebase original receipts or change the selected source/native policy.

## 3. Integrate costs by opportunity, attempt and evidenced outcome

**Existing:** percentage-cost fills are uncalibrated scenarios. The separate pure fee-provenance validator distinguishes payer/currency/refunds, refuses unknown-as-zero and prevents included-fee double counting. It is not integrated into the percentage-cost evaluator or proof of quote completeness.

**New deliverable:** a scenario/calibration schema retaining every eligible opportunity, missing quote, assembly refusal, distinct attempt, unprocessed expiry, processed failure, success and unresolved outcome. Report per-opportunity and per-completed-flow results, carrying charged failed attempts forward; success-only averages select away costs. A paper scenario can prescribe these outcomes, but must not label their probabilities measured.

[Solana's transaction pipeline](https://solana.com/docs/core/transactions/transaction-pipeline) validates blockhash age before fee deduction; processed account-loading/instruction failures can retain charges. Thus a timeout or generic failure does not establish uncharged expiration. [Current fee documentation](https://solana.com/docs/core/fees/fee-structure) charges legacy/v0 priority fees on requested CU limit, not consumption; v1 instead uses an absolute lamport priority fee. Retain transaction version, payer and exact atomic charges, then apply a timestamped conversion if reporting USD. Fixed lamport costs have a larger percentage effect at smaller notionals. Refundable storage remains distinct from permanent expense.

[Jupiter Order & Execute](https://developers.jup.ag/docs/swap/order-and-execute) separates expected quoted output from realized total input/output and describes included platform fees. [Price V3](https://developers.jup.ag/docs/price) supplies a reference mark, not an amount-specific fill. Preserve quote amount/time/route/expiry/fee inclusion, action delay, source block and eventual outcome; do not subtract quoted impact or included fees twice. Slippage tolerance is a limit, not realized slippage. Historical closes cannot recover missing counterfactual quotes or fills.

**Acceptance:** versioned lower/base/stress assumptions have explicit notional, liquidity, delay, failure and fee bounds; unknown tails remain unresolved rather than a claimed universal cost bound. Separate mechanical scenario validation from later calibration against appropriately authorized observations. This memo authorizes no signing, submission or provider acquisition. Existing Jupiter retention permission stands; no reconfirmation is required.

Sequence: freeze the family/split/cost contracts first; design independent fixture oracles next; empirical corpus and calibration collection require their separate scope and resource gates. No repository, CI, configuration, account or provider state changed. Public documentation was the only network research used; no tests, package installs or data/API-key requests ran.
